The problem with “pea-sized”

Skincare tells you to use a pea, a dime, a few drops. Your face deserves a measurement, not a metaphor.

01

Press.

Every Meso bottle has a press-and-hold pump. No squeezing, no shaking, no eyeballing.

02

Hold.

The pump dispenses the recommended amount for your whole face — calibrated per product.

03

Stop.

It stops on its own. Not too much, not too little. Just one face.
