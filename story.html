Our story

Meso means face.

Meso is a skincare company built on one simple idea: you should never have to guess how much product to use. Every bottle measures for you, every serum is matched to your marks, and every ritual starts with sensitive and combination skin in mind.

meso/ ˈmeh·so / · noun · Shona— face
01

The name

A brand named for the thing it cares for.

“Meso” comes from Shona, a language spoken in Zimbabwe, where it simply means face. We wanted a name that was short, warm and honest about what we do. There's no hidden metaphor — Meso is skincare for your face, and the name says so in the most personal way we could think of.

Say it “meh-so.” It's two syllables, easy to remember, and, we hope, the word you reach for every morning and night.

02

The problem

Skincare runs on guesswork.

Read almost any skincare label and you'll find instructions like “apply a pea-sized amount,” “use a dime-sized drop,” or “a few drops is enough.” Those are metaphors, not measurements. One person's pea is another person's grape.

Too little and a product can't do its job. Too much and you waste it — or irritate the very skin you're trying to help.

For sensitive and combination skin, that guesswork matters even more. Over-applying active serums is one of the easiest ways to end up red, tight or flaky. We think the bottle itself should solve this, not the customer.

03

The pump

Press. Hold. Stop.

Every Meso product will use a press-and-hold metered pump. You press down and hold, the pump dispenses the recommended amount for a full face, and then it stops on its own. Each product's pump is calibrated to that formula, so the cleanser and the serums each give you exactly what they should.

04

The line

Shade-matched serums, borrowed from makeup.

Makeup figured out long ago that one shade doesn't fit everyone. Skincare for dark marks still mostly treats every mark the same. Meso borrows makeup's language and matches serums to the color of your marks, not the color of your skin.

Serum No. 1

Red Marks

For the pink, red or purple-toned marks that can linger after a breakout. Designed to calm visible redness while being gentle on sensitive skin.

Serum No. 2

Brown Marks

For brown or dark marks and uneven tone that stay long after a spot heals. Designed to help even things out over time.

Both sit alongside The Cleanser, a gentle daily wash for all skin types. Together they make up the first edit: three products, three pumps, one simple ritual. Meso is designed for every skin type, and we're starting with the people who've been told to “be careful” the most — sensitive and combination skin.

05

Meso Scan

An AI that reads your skin, then picks your products.

Med spas use skin-analysis machines to map redness, marks and dryness across your face. We think that kind of insight shouldn't require an appointment. Meso Scan is the AI layer we're building: take a photo, and the scanner identifies problem areas such as redness, acne marks and dry patches.

From there, Meso Scan recommends the right products for what it finds — Red Marks, Brown Marks, or both — and builds your ritual. Combined with the metered pump, that means you'll know what to use and exactly how much.
